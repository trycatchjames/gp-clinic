# Product vision

## What the platform is

The platform is the shared operational and clinical workspace for an Australian general practice. It supports a patient from registration and booking, through arrival and consultation, to internal billing, results follow-up and continuing care. It serves independent and group practices with multiple practitioners, disciplines, rooms and locations.

It is not a generic hospital record, a specialist practice package or a US insurance system. Its centre of gravity is longitudinal, person-centred primary care: rapid access to a trustworthy health summary, safe episodic documentation, continuity across practitioners, and reliable follow-up.

## Who it is for

The primary users are GPs, nurses, receptionists, practice managers and system administrators. Allied health practitioners may use the clinical workspace within their scope and assigned permissions. Patients are represented in Version 1 but do not have a portal.

## Problems it solves

- reception can operate a busy, multi-practitioner appointment book without losing the history of changes;
- clinicians can establish the correct patient context and see allergies, medicines, active problems and outstanding follow-up before acting;
- a consultation can produce an intelligible, attributable and durable clinical record without forcing unnecessary structure;
- investigations, results, recalls and tasks have explicit responsibility and cannot disappear through ambiguous “read” states;
- the practice can separate clinical access from administrative work while retaining accountable, audited collaboration;
- internal invoices, payments and claim preparation can be completed without coupling the core record to an external payer.

## What excellent software feels like

During a consultation it is immediate, stable and keyboard-efficient. At reception it is dense, forgiving and explicit about conflicts. Safety-relevant context is visible without alarm fatigue. Destructive or irreversible actions are exceptional and unmistakable. Users always know which patient, practitioner, location and working date they are acting in, and whether important work was saved.

## Outcome goals

- ordinary patient search, booking and note entry remain fast under a full clinic workload;
- no successful-looking interaction can lose or misattribute clinical information;
- every consequential action has an accountable actor, time, patient/practice context and outcome;
- incomplete clinical obligations remain visible to an assigned person or governed queue;
- the system remains usable during partial failures and fails closed for unsafe mutations;
- Australian language, date/time handling, provider concepts and billing mental models are native rather than adapted.
