# Personas

## General practitioner

Works in short, interruption-prone sessions and needs a trustworthy summary before recording assessment and plan. May cover another practitioner's results or recalls. Needs efficient free text with optional structure, clear authorship, rapid orders/referrals/prescribing and explicit completion.

## Practice nurse

Manages observations, immunisations, care-plan activities, patient education and delegated follow-up within scope. Often moves between an assigned worklist, waiting room and consultation. Needs the responsible clinician and delegation boundaries to remain visible.

## Receptionist

Coordinates phones, arrivals, bookings, cancellations, patient details, communications and billing while protecting privacy in a public environment. Needs strong search, duplicate warnings, minimal exposure to clinical content and recovery from interrupted tasks. Must escalate clinical urgency rather than diagnose it.

## Practice manager

Configures locations, appointment types, fees, workforce access and operational policies; monitors unresolved work, debtors, quality indicators and audit exceptions. May have broad administrative authority without unrestricted clinical-note access.

## Allied health practitioner

Uses the parts of the record relevant to their care and scope, records encounters and may create tasks or referrals. Prescribing and billing authority are independent permissions, not inferred from this persona.

## System administrator

Maintains users, authentication and technical configuration. Has no automatic right to read clinical content. Emergency technical access is separately authorised, time-bounded and audited.

## Patient or authorised representative

Provides identity, demographic, consent and contact information; receives care and communications; may request access or correction. A representative's authority, scope and expiry are recorded and checked. There is no self-service portal in Version 1.
