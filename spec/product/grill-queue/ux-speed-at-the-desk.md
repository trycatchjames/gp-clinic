# Speed at the desk

## Decide

- Which tasks get keyboard shortcuts, and how people discover them.
- Whether there is a quick-find or command bar for patients and screens.
- How dense front-desk screens are, compared with the compact defaults elsewhere.
- Which flows must be possible without a mouse.

## Why we're asking

Receptionists work while on the phone, often with a patient at the desk as well. Every extra
click costs time and attention. The receptionist questions in
[open questions](../../research/open-questions.md) ask about calendar density and keyboard
flows, and the answers shape the appointment book and patient search more than any other
screen.

## Examples

- A patient rings to book. How many keystrokes from "who is calling?" to a booked appointment?
- A receptionist needs a patient's record while on another screen. Is there one shortcut that
  finds a patient from anywhere?
- The appointment book shows 15 practitioners for one day. How much fits without scrolling?
