# Research sources

**Research cut-off:** 26 August 2026. URLs were checked on that date unless an access date is shown. Australian legislation, programme rules, schedules and software change; reverify time-sensitive claims before implementation or clinical use.

Source IDs in brackets are used by the specification. Primary and regulator sources establish requirements. Vendor help establishes common mental models only.

## Standards, safety and professional duties

### RACGP

- **[RACGP-SGP5]** Royal Australian College of General Practitioners, *Standards for general practices, 5th edition*, current consolidated PDF available in 2026. [Source](https://www.racgp.org.au/getattachment/ece472a7-9a15-4441-b8e5-be892d4ffd77/Standards-for-general-practices-5th-edition.aspx). Key basis: patient communications and cost information; three-identifier verification (C6.1); health-record system, confidentiality and information security (C6.2–C6.4); consultation and summary content (C7.1, QI2.1); medicines and follow-up systems (GP2.1–GP2.2); appointments and access (GP1.1). The source explicitly says Medicare number is not an approved patient identifier.
- **[RACGP-HIGHRISK]** RACGP, *High-risk results identified outside normal opening hours*, Standards fact sheet, available 2026. [Source](https://www.racgp.org.au/FSDEDEV/media/documents/Running%20a%20practice/Practice%20standards/5th%20edition/Managing-high-risk-results-outside-normal-opening-hours.pdf). Requires a documented process and a reachable ordering/delegated practitioner or after-hours arrangement.
- **[RACGP-SEX-GENDER]** RACGP, *Collecting and recording information about patient sex, gender, variations of sex characteristics and sexual orientation*, Standards fact sheet. [Source](https://www.racgp.org.au/FSDEDEV/media/documents/Running%20a%20practice/Practice%20standards/5th%20edition/Collecting-and-recording-information-about-patient-sex-gender.pdf). Supports separate, private, respectful collection of assigned sex at birth, gender, pronouns and variations of sex characteristics.
- **[RACGP-RECORDS]** RACGP, *Quality health records in general practice*, current web guide. [Source](https://www.racgp.org.au/running-a-practice/practice-resources/general-practice-guides/quality-health-records-in-general-practice/introduction). Supports accurate, complete and maintained records for clinical decisions, continuity, audit and quality improvement.
- **[RACGP-PRIVACY]** RACGP, *Privacy and managing health information in general practice*, revised 24 May 2023. [Source](https://www.racgp.org.au/running-a-practice/security/managing-practice-information/privacy-of-health-information). Applies the Privacy Act, APPs and relevant state/territory health-record laws to general practice.
- **[RACGP-INFOSEC]** RACGP, *Information security in general practice*, available 2026. [Source](https://www.racgp.org.au/running-a-practice/security/protecting-your-practice-information/information-security-in-general-practice). Covers governance, access, business continuity, third parties, backups, secure destruction and recovery.
- **[RACGP-FINANCIAL-CONSENT]** RACGP, *Informed financial consent*, available 2026. [Source](https://www.racgp.org.au/running-a-practice/practice-resources/general-practice-guides/informed-financial-consent). Basis for giving clear expected-cost information before care where practicable.

### Other Australian clinical and professional authorities

- **[MBA-GMP]** Medical Board of Australia, *Good medical practice: a code of conduct for doctors in Australia*, effective 1 October 2020. [Source](https://www.medicalboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines-Policies/Code-of-conduct.aspx). Section 10.5 requires accurate, secure, timely records sufficient for continuity and dates changes/additions to electronic records.
- **[ACSQHC-PRIMARY]** Australian Commission on Safety and Quality in Health Care, *Primary and Community Healthcare Clinical Safety Standard*, current 2026. [Source](https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/national-standards/primary-and-community-healthcare-standards/clinical-safety-standard). Supports three identifiers, correct care matching, available records and effective clinical communication.
- **[ACSQHC-MEDICATION]** Australian Commission on Safety and Quality in Health Care, *Medication Safety Standard—documentation of medicine allergies and adverse drug reactions*, current 2026. [Source](https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/standards/nsqhs-standards/medication-safety-standard/documentation-patient-information/action-408). Supports structured allergy/ADR history and visibility during medicine use.
- **[IMM-HANDBOOK]** Australian Government Department of Health, Disability and Ageing, *Australian Immunisation Handbook—After vaccination*, updated 22 June 2026. [Source](https://immunisationhandbook.health.gov.au/after-vaccination). Specifies local clinical-file documentation including vaccine, batch, dose, administration date/time and site and covers AEFI records.
- **[ADHA-GP-CIS]** Australian Digital Health Agency Implementer Hub, *Clinical Information System Requirements—General Practitioner Recommended minimum software requirements v1.0*, DH-4211:2025, updated 6 July 2026. [Source](https://implementer.digitalhealth.gov.au/resources/clinical-information-system-standards-general-practitioner-recommended-minimum-software-requirements-v1-0). Relevant as a current Australian GP CIS baseline, particularly future interoperability; Version 1 does not implement its external exchange requirements.

## Privacy, security and accessibility

- **[OAIC-HEALTH]** Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, *Guide to health privacy*, version 2.0, updated 9 May 2025. [Source](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/health-service-providers/guide-to-health-privacy). Covers collection notices/consent, use and disclosure, access, correction and health management activities.
- **[OAIC-APPS]** OAIC, *Australian Privacy Principles*. [Source](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles). The 13 APPs cover collection, governance, use/disclosure, integrity, correction and access.
- **[OAIC-JURISDICTIONS]** OAIC, *State and territory privacy legislation*, updated 1 December 2025. [Source](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-legislation/state-and-territory-privacy-legislation). Notes that private health providers are covered by federal law and may also be covered by NSW, Victoria or ACT health privacy law.
- **[OAIC-NDB]** OAIC, *Quick reference guide for responding to data breaches*, published 29 June 2026. [Source](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/notifiable-data-breaches/quick-reference-guide-for-responding-to-data-breaches). Confirms private health providers are in the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme and supports contain, assess, notify and review workflows.
- **[ACSC-E8]** Australian Signals Directorate/Australian Cyber Security Centre, *Essential Eight explained* and assessment guidance. [Source](https://www.cyber.gov.au/business-government/asds-cyber-security-frameworks/essential-eight/essential-eight-explained). Basis for MFA, privilege restriction, patching, hardening and protected/tested backups.
- **[WCAG22]** W3C, *Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2*, Recommendation 12 December 2024. [Source](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/). Technology-neutral accessibility baseline; this specification requires Level AA.

## Billing and provider concepts

- **[SA-MBS-BILLING]** Services Australia, *MBS and DVA billing*, updated 8 September 2025. [Source](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/mbs-and-dva-billing?context=20). Establishes itemised-account content, patient/claimant distinction and use of the provider number for the place where service was provided.
- **[DOH-BULKBILL]** Australian Government Department of Health, Disability and Ageing, *Bulk billing—information for patients*, updated 23 January 2026. [Source](https://www.health.gov.au/bulkbilling/for-patients). Bulk billing means the practitioner accepts the Medicare benefit as full payment; private/mixed billing may require patient payment and later benefit claim.
- **[DOH-AOB]** Australian Government Department of Health, Disability and Ageing, *Assignment of Medicare Benefits for Bulk Billing—Frequently Asked Questions*, published 24 August 2026. [Source](https://www.health.gov.au/resources/publications/assignment-of-medicare-benefits-for-bulk-billing-frequently-asked-questions). Current context for future assignment-of-benefit work; no external assignment flow is specified in Version 1.
- **[MBS-ONLINE]** Australian Government, *Medicare Benefits Schedule Online*. [Source](https://www.mbsonline.gov.au/). Authoritative time-varying item descriptors and fees. Version 1 may retain a dated local snapshot but cannot claim catalogue currency.

## Established-product workflow observations

These are not authorities and no interface, text or proprietary rule is copied.

- **[BP-WAITING]** Best Practice Software, *Waiting Room*, current help viewed August 2026. [Source](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/oxford/Management/AppointmentBook/WaitingRoom.htm). Shows the familiar movement from arrived to with practitioner and billing, plus scheduled/arrived/wait time and multi-location context.
- **[BP-VISIT]** Best Practice Software, *Preview notes, start and finalise a visit*, updated 14 February 2025. [Source](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/oxford/Clinical/PatientRecord/StartVisit.htm). Demonstrates the useful separation between previewing a record and starting an encounter.
- **[BP-RESULTS]** Best Practice Software, *Manage Pathology and Imaging Results*, current help viewed August 2026. [Source](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/spectra/Clinical/PathologyandRadiology/ManageResults.htm). Demonstrates practitioner inbox, patient matching, action and recall creation.
- **[BP-ALLERGIES]** Best Practice Software, *Allergies and adverse drug reactions*, current help viewed August 2026. [Source](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/spectra/Clinical/PatientRecord/AllergiesReaction.htm). Demonstrates prominent allergy/ADR display and explicit “nil known”.
- **[BP-REMINDERS]** Best Practice Software, *Reminders and Actions* and *Clinical reminders*, current help viewed August 2026. [Source](https://kb.bpsoftware.net/bppremier/spectra/InstallAndUpgrade/RemindersAndActions.htm). Shows distinct staff to-dos/actions, patient reminder worklists, consented channels and contact history.
- **[MD-APPTS]** MedicalDirector, *Appointments (Helix)*, current help viewed August 2026. [Source](https://www.medicaldirector.com/help/topics-helix/Appointments.htm). Shows day/week books, multiple schedules, variable duration, unavailable time, provisional patients and DNA.
- **[MD-RESULTS]** MedicalDirector, *Message Centre—Inbox*, current help viewed August 2026. [Source](https://www.medicaldirector.com/help/topics-helix/Message_Centre_Inbox.htm). Shows matched/unmatched queues, assignment, explicit result dispositions and timeline provenance.
- **[MD-TIMELINE]** MedicalDirector, *Patient Timeline*, current help viewed August 2026. [Source](https://www.medicaldirector.com/help/topics-helix/Patient_Timeline.htm). Demonstrates a filterable longitudinal record and sensitivity markers.
- **[ZED-RESULTS]** Zedmed, *Managing the Results Inbox*, current help viewed August 2026. [Source](https://help.zedmed.com.au/help/pdfexport/id/6152ad3eef4687e55d7b24d4). Demonstrates result matching, clinician review and linked follow-up action.
- **[ZED-WAITING]** Zedmed, *Manage the Waiting Room*, current help viewed August 2026. [Source](https://help.zedmed.com.au/help/pdfexport/id/61248707d328a988697b23da). Demonstrates appointment-to-waiting-room operations.
- **[GENIE-APPTS]** Genie Solutions, *Appointments* and *Using the Appointments Book*, public Version 7 manual, viewed August 2026. [Source](https://genie-v7-manual.geniesolutions.com.au/manual/v7/HTML/appointments.htm). Shows a shared receptionist/clinician appointment surface and direct transitions from an appointment to the clinical record, waiting room and invoicing. The manual describes an older product version, so it is evidence of an established mental model rather than current product capability.
- **[GENIE-CLINICAL]** Genie Solutions, *Clinical*, public Version 7 manual, viewed August 2026. [Source](https://genie-v7-manual.geniesolutions.com.au/manual/v7/HTML/clinical.htm). Shows a dense patient workspace with prominent access to notes, investigations, referrals and recalls. The age caveat for GENIE-APPTS also applies.
- **[MEDIRECORDS-RESULTS]** MediRecords, *Secure Messaging Inbox for Results: User Guide*, updated 13 August 2026. [Source](https://support.medirecords.com/hc/en-us/articles/16913974687887-Secure-Messaging-Inbox-for-Results-User-Guide). Demonstrates results remaining in a provider inbox until formally reviewed and closed, with linked task, clinical-action and appointment entry points. External messaging is outside Version 1.
- **[MEDIRECORDS-DASHBOARD]** MediRecords, *Patient Dashboard*, current help viewed August 2026. [Source](https://support.medirecords.com/hc/en-us/articles/13432658823567-Patient-Dashboard). Demonstrates an at-a-glance patient context with direct actions for appointment, consultation, task and account workflows.

## Secondary risk-management sources

- **[AVANT-RECORDS]** Avant, *Medical records—the essentials*, available 2026. [Source](https://avant.org.au/resources/medical-records-the-essentials). Supports additive, timestamped correction of clinical notes while preserving original information. Used as supporting guidance, not law.

## Source use cautions

- RACGP accreditation requirements are not themselves a complete legal specification.
- ACSQHC standards may cover a wider primary/community setting; this specification uses compatible safety practices without claiming accreditation against a different scheme.
- Vendor behaviour demonstrates familiarity, not correctness. Where a vendor permits deletion or conflates terms, this specification follows the safety/domain rule instead.
- State/territory medicines, privacy and retention rules differ. The product must be jurisdiction-configurable and legally reviewed before clinical deployment.
